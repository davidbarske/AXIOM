ENGINEERING PRINCIPLES

Bound the problem

One clear proof threshold before expanding scope.

Instrument the build

Data and test evidence over intuition alone.

Design for iteration

Reusable modules, traceable decisions and clear documentation.

Gate safety and scale

No product claims before testing, certification and liability are understood.
